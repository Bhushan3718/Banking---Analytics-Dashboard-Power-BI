# 🏦 Banking Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI Banking Analytics Dashboard** that provides valuable insights into customer demographics, loan performance, account types, credit card usage, and customer retention through dynamic visualizations and KPIs.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Banking Analytics Dashboard** is designed to help banks analyze customer data, monitor loan performance, and understand customer behavior through an interactive dashboard. It enables stakeholders to make data-driven decisions using visual reports and key performance indicators (KPIs).

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Banks deal with a large volume of customer, loan, and account data. Analyzing this information manually is time-consuming and inefficient. This dashboard provides a centralized view of banking operations, allowing users to monitor customer activity, loan status, account distribution, and customer retention effectively.

## 💡 Business Insights

- The dashboard analyzes data from **1,800 banking customers**.
- Active and inactive customers are almost equally distributed.
- Loan approval rate is significantly higher than rejected applications.
- Savings accounts have the highest customer share.
- Visa cards are the most preferred card type.
- Customer distribution is spread across multiple Indian states.
- Male and Female customers have nearly similar average credit scores.
- Engineers and Students represent a major share of customers.
